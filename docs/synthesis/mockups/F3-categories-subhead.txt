WHERE THE ANTI-MIDDLEMAN FRAMING GOES INSTEAD:
The "בלי מתווכים" idea is good (Sub 2 Pattern 4 — anti-
middleman framing works). It just doesn't belong on the
category grid where it's saturated. Move it to:
- /about values strip (already half-present: "שקיפות" +
  "קרבה" values do this work)
- Founder strip (F7 post-launch)

- Sub 2 Anti-pattern 1: 5/7 Israeli sites use the
  "מהחקלאי לצרכן" formula in their <title> element.
- Sub 2 Pattern 4: farmdirect.co.il's "ללא פערי תיווך"
  is the sharper version — but they're a single farm,
  not a directory. Lane is open for mehamakor.
- Sub 1 §3.2 C4: subhead is transactional ("בלי מתווכים"
  is a Wolt-style guarantee, not a magazine description).
