3 patterns used

  1. 1Published verification criteria as a public trust surface. The qualifying-document matrix is shown openly, by category — the F9 open lane (no Israeli competitor publishes criteria). — CrowdFarming "How we verify our farms" · GrownBy "Approval criteria"
  2. 2Affirmative absence — explain the missing badge, never flag it. A calm block states why a business may have no badge (legally exempt category), so absence reads as lawful, not suspect. — Yelp Verified License FAQ (relative-perception mitigation, Saeedi 2023)
  3. 3Editorial numerals + ink-on-paper restraint. Cormorant gold numerals as magazine devices, hairline rules, flat cream canvas, gold as sparing accent — the house voice carried into a content-dense page. — NYT Cooking · Kinfolk

2 anti-patterns avoided

  • ✕No two competing tier cards · no "unverified" label. Tiers are not pitted side-by-side as winner/loser; מוצהר is the baseline path and is never negatively marked. The badge marks an extra document, not extra trust.
  • ✕No AI-slop, no over-claim. No Inter/Roboto, no purple gradients, no shadcn cards, no Lucide, no stock photos, no emoji — Phosphor regular only. Copy never claims "we check every license" (over-claim under ADR-022): personal vetting for all, document check = מאומת tier only.
