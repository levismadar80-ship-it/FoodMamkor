The system — one scrim, three slots, layered cream

Photography enters at exactly three points. Everywhere else, depth comes from the cream itself: grain 0.035, tonal steps to background-alt with a hand-cut seam, and the plate's offset panel. The hero is the only place text ever sits over an image.

The contrast-scrim token

--scrim-ink: linear-gradient( to top, rgb(28 26 23 / .88) 0%, rgb(28 26 23 / .80) 44%, rgb(28 26 23 / .40) 66%, rgb(28 26 23 / 0) 86%); /* ink = --site-text. token-derived, no new hue. bottom-anchored. */

AA assumption — stated: worst case is a 100%-white blown highlight directly under the text, on a busy composition — NOT a smooth gradient. The hero mock below renders a deliberately bright/busy test fill to prove it.
· sub + search + CTAs zone (bottom 44%, α ≥ .80): composite vs cream ≥ 8.0 : 1 — AA + AAA
· H1 top line (may reach ~50% on 4:5 mobile, α ≥ .70): ≥ 6.0 : 1 — large-text AA ×2 margin
· everything else in the system: text on cream/alt at token defaults — no overlay anywhere.

Depth on cream — between the photos

  • 1Grain 0.035, global — the shipped /about recipe, one layer, over photography too.
  • 2Tonal steps with a hand-cut seam. --background → --background-alt transitions get a torn-paper edge (CSS mask, ~4px amplitude) — DS signature gesture №4. The cream folds; nothing floats.
  • 3The plate's offset panel — every framed photo carries a background-alt rectangle offset behind its mat: depth by overlap, zero shadows.
  • 4Overlap moments — search card rides the hero seam; /about portrait column keeps S8 D sticky behavior.
  • 5Ken Burns on the hero only — 22s, ≤ 1.06. prefers-reduced-motion → static frame; #1053 motion layer untouched.
