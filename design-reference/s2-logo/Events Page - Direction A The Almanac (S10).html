C

Patterns & anti-patterns

Three house patterns reused from the S1–S9 lineage, two slop traps actively avoided.

3 + 2
cited precedents
avoided traps

Patterns used

1
The editorial date device. The date-rail is the events-page form of the brand's signature oversized-numeral gesture — Frank Ruhl 900 day numbers riding a colored tick. S2 signature gesture #2 · scale logic from S3 Hero.
2
Species-by-accent, not by layout. Events (green) and experiences (gold) share one skeleton and diverge only on tick + eyebrow + vocabulary — the same available/verified chip-color discipline that distinguishes states on S6 Business / ProducerCard.
3
Two-tab + view-toggle chrome. Tab pair and the rows/calendar segmented toggle reuse the pill + segmented-control vocabulary from S4 Floating Navbar (MEH-732); empty-state action ladder ports from S9 Login.

Anti-patterns avoided

1
Avoided: the cookie-cutter image-top / text-bottom event card. Instead: the date leads every row; A is type-led and survives with zero photography — no generic stock-photo dependence, no failed-image gray boxes.
2
Avoided: raw emoji as UI glyphs (📅 🌱 in the legacy empty state). Instead: Phosphor calendar-x / cooking-pot in gold, regular/bold weight — never duotone, never Lucide, per the icon migration + brand lock.
