Patterns & anti-patterns.

What S9 carries in from S1–S8, the two traps it deliberately sidesteps, and the locked decisions returned for review.

Lock self-check.

S9 introduces no new tokens and no new primitives — it reassembles S7's vocabulary into the second door. Three reused patterns (with precedent), two anti-patterns avoided, and the open items.

Patterns used — cited

✓Social-first auth order — Google → Apple → או → email + password. Precedent: S7 step-00 account gate (re-synced to social-first 2026-06-05). Two doors, one order.
✓Warm editorial error — Cormorant italic on --accent-warm, field tinted 5%, never red. Precedent: S7 state-a field errors.
✓Cross-link door panel — --light tile, circle glyph, text-link only, demoted below the CTA. Precedent: S7 .rgc-cross producer↔consumer link.
✓Carried chrome — FloatingNavbar v5, gold eyebrow rule, labels-above 54px fields, green .btn-primary with the ← arrow. Precedent: S5/S6/S7.

Anti-patterns avoided

✕"Another app" card-on-cream — no floating white auth card, no default-shadcn shell. Open fields sit on the cream paper like the rest of the brand.
✕Red browser-default validation — no shouting :invalid red, no native error bubbles. Semantics via aria-invalid; tone via warm italic.

Resolved this round

✓Headline locked — טוב לראות אותך שוב. ברוכה השבה rejected — gendered, fails ADR-014.
✓Combined release — S9 + S7-v4.1 both gated by MEH-742; the two doors flip to social-first together in one approval pass.
