Design thesis.
2+4

Two heroes,
four balance.

The brief offered 2+3 or 1+4. With six categories not five, both ratios needed an honest extension; I chose 2 + 4 as the cleanest reading of the 2+3 intent — two cards above the fold given editorial weight, four supporting cards beneath them on equal footing. The two heroes are not "the most important categories" — they are the two that, this season, the editor is leading with: חלב וגבינות and לחמים ואפייה. Tomorrow the heroes can rotate. The shape of the page does not.

In RTL, asymmetry has to travel right-to-left. The hero pair lands at the start (right) of row one, where the reader's eye is already settled after the H2; row two then unfolds across to the end (left), faster, lighter. This is the rhythm of a magazine spread — opening shot, then a strip of indices below. It is also why the hero glyph cells are 16:9, and the small ones are 1:1: the eye expects a cinematic frame at the start and an inventory at the end.

Hover state intentionally avoids shadow lift — borders shift to --green, and a gold hairline appears under the category name. The cards do not float; they are pages of a book.

Reference. Cereal magazine, category index (issue 17+ format) — opening leading-shot pair followed by the smaller browse strip. Selected over Kinfolk (whose category indices lean uniform-grid) and Natoora (whose category surface is e-commerce-shaped, not editorial).

Family of marks

hand-drawn family

Category glyphs share visual DNA with the S2 wordmark mark — same hand, same strokeWidth idiom. The m + dot is the lockup; the category glyphs are its kin.

  • — hero stroke 2px · supporting 1.5px
  • — hero glyph 140px · supporting 80px
  • — gold accent role is flexible by design — anatomical (eye on דג, holes in גבינה), functional (steam on לחם, drip on ירקות), or signature (highlight on בקבוק שמן, leaf-fruit on סבון). Family cohesion lives in stroke + line style, not in accent role.
  • — NOT Phosphor · NOT emoji · NOT botanical illustration

Decision Option B per Sapir v4 review. Glyph family ships at LOCK with documented variability — not deferred, not pretended-uniform.

future migration

Glyphs are scaffold — they live until photography lands.

  • Trigger: 50+ producers AND 6 producer photos on hand.
  • Swap: 1 : 1. Frame structure unchanged · glyph-cell becomes photo-cell · same 16:9/1:1 aspect ratios.
  • Owner: MEH-602 (media integration).

Hover

single signal (v7)
  • — border --border → --green
  • — no CTA text · no arrow · no underline
  • — no glyph scale · no shadow lift
  • — transition 200ms ease on border-color only

Single signal: border only. Card is the button — magazine convention. Hover teaches affordance silently. Diverges from ProducerCard P2 v4 (border + underline) because each category card already carries an editorial numeral that anchors the eye.

meta strip (v8)

Center-aligned numeral + category name. Top padding 24px (hero 32px) on 8-grid.

Separated from glyph zone — illustration cards use bar-pattern, not over-art. Cream base lacks depth for integrated label feel, so the honest treatment is the separated bar (Vignelli / Cereal precedent).

eyebrow (v6)

Section indicator only. No count, no inventory.

Industry pattern: magazine eyebrows are wayfinding, not metrics. Cereal / Kinfolk / The Gentlewoman use numbers only for issue / volume ("Issue 28", "nº 33") — never for section counts.

punctuation (v6)

Section H2 has no terminal period. Period reserved for the Hero's declarative thesis ("אוכל מקומי. במקום אחד.") — navigation prompts read as invitations, not assertions.

02 grid discipline

Vignelli.

  • 8px baseline grid holds across all spacing · verified in §Grid math (lines 1058–1095). no notes.
  • Hero/supporting ratio: glyph 140:80 = 1.75:1; stroke 2:1.5 = 1.33:1. Proportional but loose, not mathematical. accepted as hand-drawn family loose-by-design.
  • Card padding consistent per BP, hero+1 step over supporting (24/32 vs 20/24). Clean.
  • Section padding 48/32/24 across BPs — generous, editorial.
03 mark-making · craft

Draplin.

  • Family cohesion: stroke + line style — not accent role. Six glyphs share strokeWidth idiom and clean line geometry; gold accent role is functional (eye, hole, drip, highlight). → v4 decision: Option B · documented in §Family of marks as conscious variability.
  • Gold accent: flexible by design. Fish eye = anatomical · cheese holes = anatomical · bread steam = functional · carrot drip = functional · bottle highlight = signature · soap leaf-fruit = signature. Three roles, one palette. Locked, not deferred.
  • Hand-drawn imperfection feels geometric, not inky. Truly hand-drawn would have wobble. deferred — SVG-clean is acceptable scaffold.
  • Stroke ratio 2:1.5 reads at scale; not bold. Could push hero to 2.5 if reviewer wants more contrast.
04 editorial restraint

Park.

  • Section earns its place — it is the navigation surface, not filler. no notes.
  • "גלי לפי קטגוריה" + sub — magazine voice; no SaaS smell.
  • Eyebrow "קטגוריות · 06" reads editorial-functional. The hairline rule before it does the magazine-section work.
  • Editorial push deferred post-Cohort #1. Cereal section heads sometimes set the eyebrow vertically or use an oversized leading initial. Mine is straight. Current restraint level matches Cohort #1 producer count · magazine-tier typography unlocks post-launch. → v4 decision: Option B · documented in §Editorial restraint as conscious gap.
05 anti-marketplace · honesty

Chimero.

  • Counters removed in v5. Earlier "מתחילות עכשיו" fallback resolved the cold-start case; v5 removes the surface entirely. Magazine convention (Natoora · Cereal · Infatuation) wins over the fallback. DNA-correct.
  • Hierarchy preserved: category name (H3 22–32px) > numeral (italic 22px) > count (caps 12px). Never inverted.
  • "בתי עסק" used everywhere; zero "מוכרים" / "יצרנים" leaks.
  • Card is <a> to category page · no "buy now" affordance. Anti-Etsy.
06 state tokens · system

Frost.

  • Hover treatment in P3 diverges from ProducerCard P2 v4: border only, no underline. Reason documented §Hover — each card carries an editorial numeral that already anchors the eye, underline would duplicate.
  • Border tokens semantic: --border (rest) → --green (hover). Correct.
  • Selected state undefined. If categories double as filter chips in the producer-list view (likely), we need .cat.is-selected. flagged → S4 integration scope.
  • Family-of-marks ties documented in inspector but invisible to user — that’s correct (signal is for designers; reader sees only the glyphs).
