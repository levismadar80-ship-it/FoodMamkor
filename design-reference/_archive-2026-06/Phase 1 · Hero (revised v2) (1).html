Photo-led notes — contrast & gradient.

Cream text on the photo demands AA-pass contrast. The bottom-only gradient (cream 0% → --green-dark 88%) covers the lower 75% of the frame so H1 + Sub never sit on raw image.

Gradient Single direction (180°), four stops: 0/25/65/88%. No radial orb. No glass over the page itself.
Nav over photo Cream text + cream hairline border. Account pill outlined in cream. This is the "State 2" treatment that becomes Floating Pill State 2 in P4.
Search elevation Warm-white 95% opacity card with a single dropshadow — the only floating surface. Not glassmorphism — full opacity card on photo.
Near-me on photo Outlined cream pill with 6px backdrop-blur over a translucent green tint. Single ingredient, not the meal.
Image PLACEHOLDER · golden-hour sourdough. Final image: warm, specific to a Cohort #1 producer — never "hands holding vegetables".
Reduced motion No Ken Burns by default at 375; mobile photo is static. Desktop adds a slow drift only if prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference.
