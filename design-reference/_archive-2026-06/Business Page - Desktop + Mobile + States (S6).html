MEH-76 audit fixes

✓Identity, photo-first. Hero leads with real photography; the no-photo fallback is a deliberate monogram מ over a faint category glyph — never the broken name.slice(0,2) → גב.
✓One status surface. Open/closed lives only in the hours block; vacation collapses to a single muted banner. Never two banners at once.
✓One primary CTA. A single green channel button. Share is demoted to a text-and-icon action שתפי — never a second green button.
✓Ruthless hierarchy. The contact card holds three things — CTA, channel list, share — not nine stacked elements.
✓Location in content flow. The warm S5 mini-map sits in the Practical section with Waze / Google buttons, not buried in a sidebar.

Design-system lock

✓Editorial green only. Every CTA is --primary #2E6853 — never WhatsApp brand green, even on the WhatsApp button. Gold #8B6914 carries section rules, pull-quote marks, the map marker ring.
✓Type. Frank Ruhl Libre 900 masthead + pull-quote; DM Sans body; Cormorant italic accents. No Hebrew over 24px in DM Sans.
✓Navbar v5 embedded as-is: filled חיפוש, outlined הוסיפו עסק, text כניסה לחשבון, links גלו · מפה · אודות.
✓.numeric LTR-isolation on every number, phone, time-range and distance (direction: ltr; unicode-bidi: isolate) — the en-dash in 08:00–18:00 never flips.
✓Voice. Feminine throughout, בית עסק never יצרן, zero emojis, no balloon words. RTL logical properties; borders 1px --border; touch targets ≥ 44px.
✓Positioning. הבית הראשון של העסק שלך — a magazine profile (story + pull-quote), not a marketplace listing.
✓Story is optional. No drop cap (a Latin import); opening words emphasised in Frank Ruhl 900 instead. Pull-quote drops cleanly when absent, and the whole הסיפור section disappears for story-less businesses — never a placeholder.
