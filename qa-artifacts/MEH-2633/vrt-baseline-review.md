## Scope control

`playwright --list` was used to resolve the grep before dispatching: it selects
**exactly 6 tests in 1 file** — `about` / `login` / `register` × `desktop` /
`mobile`. The workflow's only knob is a `--grep`, so it cannot filter by
project; the 2 passing desktop baselines were therefore in scope too.

**They came back byte-identical and were not committed.** That is the control
that makes the rest of this review evidence rather than hope: the regen
demonstrably ran, was deterministic, and did not disturb a passing baseline

## What changed, per PNG

**`about-mobile`, `register-mobile` — clean.** Everything above the footer is
byte-identical (`ImageChops.difference(...).getbbox() is None` over the top
7236px / 1433px). The only change is the footer, +52px.

**`login-mobile` — clean, plus one benign 0.054% region.** Same +52px footer.
One additional 18×18px diff at (346,234)–(364,252): the **Google "G" logo**,
same glyph, different sub-pixel antialiasing from the remote GSI asset. 324 of
595,431 px = 0.054%, two orders of magnitude under the 2% tolerance, so it
cannot carry a comparison on its own.

**`login-desktop` — carried pre-existing drift that this regen absorbed.**
Dimensions are unchanged, so this one never was the canvas-dimension mismatch
the other three are. 5.40% of pixels differ, in two bands:

- `y≈1360–1400` — the footer row. This PR's change.
- `y≈160–720` — **not this PR.** The old baseline was missing the Google
  consent line (`oauth_terms_google`, `he.json:163`) that the product renders,
  and showed a muted submit button where the current render shows the full
  green one. The consent line's absence pushed the whole form block up, which
  is the entire 160–720 band.

The old **mobile** login baseline already had both the consent line and the
green button, and `register-desktop` — byte-identical after this regen — has
the consent line too. Two baselines of the same component disagreeing about
whether a line exists means one was captured from an older product state. So
`login-desktop` was stale before this branch, and would have been red here
regardless of the footer.

No history claim is made about *when* it went stale: this clone is shallow
(`git rev-parse --is-shallow-repository` → `true`), and under MEH-1519 a
shallow clone reports the graft commit as every file's origin. The conclusion
above rests only on comparing the artifacts with the current source.

Filed separately rather than widened into this PR.

## Why CC ran the regen

`workflow.md`'s hand-off table lists VRT baselines under *needs Sapir's hands*.
The rule-23 amendment (MEH-2302, 22/10) is newer and specific to merging UI
work: there is no human mobile-QA pass, CC owns UI QA end to end, must never
report "waiting on Sapir", and must land *"VRT clean or each re-baselined PNG
reviewed."* That makes CC the reviewer of re-baselined PNGs, so the regen is
CC's to drive. Recorded here because the PR body previously said the opposite.
