## Design Context

### Users
Israeli women (primary: 25–45) hunting for real food — grass-fed meat, sourdough, farm cheese, organic produce, natural skincare — directly from small producers and home cooks. The dominant UI voice is feminine (נקבה), Hebrew RTL. Context of use: a phone in the kitchen mid-week planning "what to eat / who to buy from", or a laptop in the evening browsing the map and saving favorites.

The job-to-be-done: **"find a trustworthy local producer for real food in 30 seconds, not two hours of WhatsApp group crawling."** The founder's own origin story is the archetype.

Secondary users:
- **בעלות עסק** (producer-owners) — onboarding, profile management, publishing events, tracking follows/WhatsApp clicks
- **שכנות שמבשלות בבית** (home cooks) — lightweight listings via `/neighbor`
- **Admin** — moderation queue, producer approval, reports triage

### Brand Personality
**Three words:** warm · handmade · trustworthy.

- **Voice:** feminine Hebrew, second person (`-י` verbs), conversational, never corporate. Examples from the locked micro-copy table: "חפשי ירקות טריים...", "טוענת עסקים טריים...", "הוסיפי את העסק שלך 🌿", "לא מצאנו עסקים באזור הזה — עדיין 🌱".
- **Emotions to evoke:** confidence ("I can trust what's on this plate"), calm ("I'm not drowning in tabs"), belonging ("these are my neighbors"). Never urgency, never FOMO, never gamification.
- **The litmus test (from CLAUDE.md):** *"Does this feel like a local farmers' market, or like a tech startup?"* If it's startup, simplify.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone:** organic, earthy, analog. Warm cream backgrounds (never pure white), deep forest greens, muted gold accents. Hand-drawn feel over geometric precision.
- **Light mode only.** No dark mode (explicit decision — see CLAUDE.md note that dark mode was proposed and rejected as anti-brand). Admin sidebar is the only exception: it uses `bg-primary-dark` intentionally as a "backoffice" signal.
- **References (in priority order):**
  - [gardensweetfarm.com](https://gardensweetfarm.com) — warm cream, hand-drawn icons, Ken Burns photography
  - [foraged.com/categories](https://foraged.com/categories) — earthy category grid, emphasis on provenance
  - [Graza](https://graza.co) — marquee strips, line-art icons, confident typography
  - [Simply Chocolate](https://simplychocolate.dk) — cinematic Ken Burns, custom cursor, premium restraint
- **Anti-references:**
  - **Uber Eats / Wolt** — cold, gray, transactional, feature-dense. We are the opposite.
  - **Generic SaaS dashboards** — heavy gradients, drop shadows, glassmorphism. Banned.
  - **Placehold.co placeholders** — literally banned; `next/image` blocks SVG by default and warm branded fallbacks look better anyway.
- **Colors (locked — do not drift):**

- **Typography (locked):**
  - `font-headline` = Frank Ruhl Libre (Hebrew h1/h2/h3)
  - `font-body` = DM Sans (Hebrew + English body)

### Design Principles
1. **Farmers' market, not tech startup.** If a design choice would fit in a YC batch more than in a weekend market in Tel Aviv, simplify.
2. **Feminine voice, not neutral.** Every UI string uses נקבה (feminine). Micro-copy in the locked table is canonical — match it exactly.
3. **Warm fallbacks, no placeholders.** Missing image → branded green div with 🌿 or line-art icon. Never `placehold.co`, never gray squares.
4. **Contrast via tokens, not opacity.** `text-site-text/60` fails WCAG AA on `#F5F0E8`. Use `text-site-muted` (`#5c584f`) which gives ~5.5:1.
5. **Motion is slow and purposeful.** Ken Burns at 20–25s. `fade-up ease-out` for scroll reveals. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is non-negotiable — the Ken Burns, marquee, smooth scroll, custom cursor, and AnimatedCounter all honor it.

### Accessibility commitments
- All `<input>`/`<textarea>` have `<label htmlFor>` (sometimes `sr-only`).
- `focus-visible:ring-2` on every interactive element.
- Touch targets ≥ 44×44px on mobile.
- Icon-only links/buttons have `aria-label`. Decorative SVGs have `aria-hidden="true"`.
- Status messages use `role="status" aria-live="polite"`.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables all ambient animations.
