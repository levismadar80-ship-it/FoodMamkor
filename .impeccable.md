# מהמקור — Impeccable Design Context
> Design context consumed by the impeccable skill chain. This file is derivative — refreshed 2026-07 against the apex SoT (`docs/CONTEXT.md`). Update it when the brand direction changes, not on every tweak.

## Design Context

### Users
Israeli readers (Hebrew RTL, mobile-first) hunting for real food — sourdough, farm cheese, organic produce, honey — directly from **licensed** local food businesses (בתי עסק). Mehamakor is an editorial directory: **magazine, not marketplace**. Cohort 1 is licensed businesses only; unlicensed food production where a license is legally required stays out (see `docs/CONTEXT.md` §2).

The job-to-be-done: **"find a trustworthy local food business in 30 seconds, not two hours of WhatsApp group crawling."** Context of use: a phone in the kitchen mid-week, or an evening browse of the map and saved favorites.

Secondary users:
- **בעלות/בעלי עסקים** (business owners) — onboarding, profile management, publishing events, tracking follows and WhatsApp clicks
- **Admin** — moderation queue, business approval, reports triage

### Brand Personality
**Three words:** warm · editorial · trustworthy.

- **Voice (ADR-014, refined by ADR-024 — hybrid by surface-function):** UI chrome (buttons, loading, errors, empty states) uses gerund or neutral plural ("גלו", "הוסיפו", "טעינה"); feminine address is allowed on brand-narrative surfaces (/about story, founder letter). Forbidden on every surface: slash forms ("טוענ/ת"), masculine address to the reader, and the retired producer-noun — the entity is always **"בית עסק"**, the owner **"בעלת/בעל עסק"** (generic: "בעלי עסקים").
- **Emotions to evoke:** confidence ("I can trust what's on this plate"), calm, belonging. Never urgency, never FOMO, never gamification.
- **The litmus test:** *"Does this push toward magazine or marketplace?"* If marketplace, simplify. The word "מגזין" itself never appears in UI — the editorial register is shown, not labeled (BRAND.md §4).
- **Copy guards:** over-claim guard + customer-subject test + no-jargon rule (`docs/COPY_BANK.md`). Forbidden phrasings — home-kitchen framing, partial category lists, time-stamped framing ("ISSUE 01") — are enumerated in BRAND.md §3+§7; never reintroduce them.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone:** editorial premium — warm cream surfaces (never pure white as page background), one deep forest green, restrained gold, magazine-page rhythm, generous whitespace. Flat and tonal: depth comes from cream-vs-white steps and hairline borders, never shadows.
- **Light mode only.** No dark mode. Dark green surfaces (`green-900` sheets/footers) are component-level grounds, not a theme.
- **References (BRAND.md §5 — Kinfolk lineage):** Kinfolk · Natoora · Cherry Bombe · The Infatuation · Smitten Kitchen.
- **Anti-references:** Wolt / 10bis / marketplace UIs (transactional, feature-dense); generic SaaS dashboards — gradient orbs, glassmorphism, hover shadow-lift are banned (BRAND.md §3). The pre-reset references (gardensweet, foraged) are **retired** — do not use as a north star.
- **Colors (locked — values mirror `frontend/tailwind.tokens.json`, the token truth; naming per `docs/DESIGN.md`):**
  ```
  primary               #2e6853   deep forest green — the single brand accent
  primary-dark          #2e4a2e   hover/active (greens go darker, never lighter)
  background            #f5f0e8   warm cream page surface — never pure white
  background-alt        #ede4d2   editorial tonal step for section separation
  surface               #ffffff   cards/modals/inputs on cream only, never the page
  surface-card/floating #fffefb   faint warm-white, one step above surface
  text                  #1c1a17   warm ink — not pure black
  muted                 #6b6860   secondary text, AA on cream
  fg-muted              #5c584f   the single state de-emphasis foreground (ADR-019)
  accent                #896714   warm gold — prices, premium highlights, sparingly
  honey                 #c8821e   honey category color
  gold-on-dark          #e7c88a   gold voice on dark green surfaces only
  border                #e5dfd3   warm hairline
  green-50…green-900    #eaf3de / #c8dcb3 / #6fa284 / #2e6853 / #2e4a2e / #143228
  action-primary(-hover), state-selected — semantic aliases of primary / primary-dark
  ```
- **Typography (locked):** `headline-display/-lg/-md` = Frank Ruhl Libre 900/900/700 (fallback "David Libre", Georgia, serif); `body-*` and `label-*` = DM Sans with the tokenized **Heebo** Hebrew fallback (DM Sans has no Hebrew block). Cormorant Garamond is reserved for Latin pull-quotes only (untokenized). No more than three visible weights per screen.
- **Border radius:** sm 8px (buttons/inputs) · md 12px (chips/small cards) · lg 16px (cards) · xl 20px (modals). No `rounded-full` on rectangular elements — full rounding is for genuinely circular elements only.
- **Component state (ADR-019):** opacity-on-cream (100/70/50/30) + `fg-muted` are the only state mechanisms. **No new state-color tokens** — no error/warning/slate palette; a state that can't be served this way needs an ADR superseding ADR-019 first.

### Design Principles
1. **Magazine, not marketplace.** Every design choice passes this test.
2. **Phosphor icons only for functional UI (ADR-013).** Regular weight — duotone is off-brand. Lucide is forbidden. Category glyphs are hand-drawn SVG (tier 2); editorial illustration is tier 3, post-launch.
3. **No emoji in UI, ICU strings, brand guidance, or editorial surfaces (Emoji LOCK v2, MEH-657).** Allowed only in WhatsApp outbound, share strings, and email bodies. Missing image → branded cream/green fallback with a line-art glyph, never an emoji and never a gray placeholder.
4. **RTL logical properties only.** `ms-`/`me-`, `ps-`/`pe-`, `start-`/`end-` — never `ml-`/`mr-`/`left-`/`right-` (narrow exceptions in `.claude/rules/rtl.md`). Numerals/dates isolate with `dir="ltr"`/`.numeric`.
5. **Contrast via tokens, not opacity-on-text.** WCAG 2.1 AA (≥4.5:1 body, ≥3:1 large) on cream AND white; `muted`/`fg-muted` are the de-emphasis tokens. Tokens in JSX (`text-primary`), never hard-coded hex.
6. **Motion is slow and purposeful.** `.duration-fast/base/slow` = 180/420/640ms, `.ease-quart`; Ken Burns ~20s hero-only; `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is non-negotiable.

### Accessibility commitments
- All `<input>`/`<textarea>` have `<label htmlFor>` (sometimes `sr-only`).
- `focus-visible` ring on every interactive element (`.focus-ring` utility).
- Touch targets ≥ 44×44px on mobile.
- Icon-only links/buttons have `aria-label`; decorative SVGs `aria-hidden="true"`.
- Status messages use `role="status" aria-live="polite"`.
- IS 5568 (Israeli accessibility standard) applies — Hebrew screen-reader support, RTL-correct ARIA.
